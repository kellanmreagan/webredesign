## Content oddities / inconsistencies (AGENTS.md rule 6)

1. **Hours discrepancy between the website and the menu PDF.** The live homepage states: *Sat-Thurs: 7am - 2pm / Fri: 7am - 8pm* (extended hours Friday only). The menu PDF cover states: *Sun - Thurs 7am-2pm / Fri - Sat 7am-8pm* (extended hours Friday **and** Saturday). These directly conflict on whether Saturday has extended dinner hours. No third source (e.g. Google/Facebook) was checked to break the tie, per scope. The redesign uses the **website's** stated hours (Sat-Thurs 7am-2pm, Fri 7am-8pm) since that's the page of record being redesigned, and flags this discrepancy for the business owner to confirm — Saturday dinner-hours availability is the specific fact in question.
2. **"The Story Behind Skipper's Name"** is real, unaltered business content — it appears on the menu PDF that the site's own "Menu" nav item links to, but not on the homepage itself. It is not on the current homepage HTML. Since AGENTS.md rule 4 prohibits inventing content but this text is genuine, verbatim, owner-authored copy already published by the business (just on the linked PDF rather than the HTML), it's included on the new Menu page, matching where it already lives in the source material — not fabricated, not moved from a page that didn't have it.
3. **No real "Menu" or "Contact Us" HTML page exists on the source site** — see crawl-log.md. The redesign converts the PDF-only menu into a full HTML page (all items transcribed verbatim above, PDF still linked for anyone who wants the printable/original version) and the mailto-only "Contact Us" into a real contact page with the same phone/address/email, per AGENTS.md rule 2 (thin/confusing IA — a nav item that does nothing but pop open a mail client, with no fallback for mobile users without a configured mail app, is exactly the "thin" case that rule allows modernizing) while keeping the same 3-item nav structure and every real piece of contact information.
4. **Non-content chrome omitted (rule 5):** the two decorative drop-shadow strip graphics (`dshadow_one.jpg`, `dshadow_two.jpg`), the 1×78 layout spacer image, and the "Site Design & Hosting by BJM" third-party vendor credit logo (`bjm.jpg`) — none carry business content.

Local Restaurants, Bars & Stores

Near Kenton & Trout Creek, Michigan

One of the best parts of staying at Two Rivers is the small-town charm just down the road. From hearty bar-and-grill meals and hot breakfasts to fuel stops, groceries, and trail passes, our local establishments make it easy to gear up, grab a bite, or kick back after a day of exploring. Here's a quick guide to the spots we recommend in Kenton, Trout Creek, and the surrounding towns.

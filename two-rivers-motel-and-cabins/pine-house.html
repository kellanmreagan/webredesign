The Pine House Vacation Rental

Mass City, MI

We currently have one vacation home rental located in Mass City, MI — a 4 bedroom, two bathroom home that has been completely remodeled.

Features Include

  • Plenty of off-street trailer parking
  • Master bedroom with a king bed and master bathroom
  • Two bedrooms with queen beds
  • One bedroom with three twin beds
  • Large dining and bar area
  • Large living room with a large couch
  • Helmet pegs and a large coat rack for all your outerwear gear

Check in is at 3PM — check out is at 10AM. Price ranges from $250/night to $350/night depending on the season, plus an $80 cleaning fee. Well-behaved pets are allowed for an additional $10/night.

Pet & House Policies

  • Clean up any messes inside the house
  • Pick up after your pet outside the house
  • Any damage caused by a pet will be the responsibility of the guest that rented the house
  • One night security deposit is required

Cancellations

  • Security deposit is refundable if cancelled up to 2 weeks before stay
  • Security deposit is non-refundable if cancelled within two weeks of stay
  • Full price of stay will be charged for no-call/no-show or if cancelled within 1 day of stay

Minimum stay is 2 nights; holidays may require a 3 night minimum stay.

House Rules

By making this reservation, you agree to the following:

  • No smoking or vaping in the house. Evidence of smoking or vaping will result in an additional $250 cleaning fee
  • Do not use the back patio door — we were not able to get steps in and there is a drop off
  • Do not enter the abandoned buildings at the back of the property
  • Please let us know right away if you notice something that is broken or not working properly

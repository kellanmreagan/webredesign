Sponsorships & Community Involvement

A true U.P. experience, starting where the pavement ends—and shared with the clubs, chambers, and volunteers who make our region stronger.

Two Rivers Motel & Cabins is proud to support both the Western U.P. trail system and our broader local community. We prioritize groups working directly in the Western Upper Peninsula.

Want us to Sponsor Your Club or Event?

Please call or email us at least 30 days in advance.

📞 Call us: (906) 852-3541
📧 Email us: tworiversmotelandcabins@gmail.com

What We Will Need

  • Organization name & website/social link
  • Event/cause & dates
  • Requested support (cash amount, in-kind lodging, promo, etc.)
  • How funds/items will be used
  • Recognition plan (logo placement, mentions, tickets)
  • Contact name, email, phone

How We Decide

  • Alignment with trail stewardship & outdoor access
  • Local impact within Trout Creek, Kenton, Sidnaw & the Western U.P.
  • Volunteer-driven or community-benefit initiatives
  • Timing (avoid overlap/duplication)
  • Budget available

Transparency: we'll typically post on social media about the events and publish a short year-end recap of sponsored groups and outcomes. Contact us for a high resolution media & logo kit.

Visiting Michigan's Western Upper Peninsula?

Planning a ride or hiking nearby? We'll help you choose the best routes and must-see spots, stage your vehicle, get food and groceries, and even hold your mail or packages for pickup.

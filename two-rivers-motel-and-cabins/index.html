Guest Testimonials

What Visitors Say

★★★★★

“We had a great time at Two Rivers! We rented the "Fishing Cabin" and it was perfect. We are planning another trip late this fall and will probably book again if it's available. Very nice people, clean, quiet and just far enough out. Would definitely recommend this place to anyone looking to stay in the area.”

— Kim K., July 2025
★★★★★

“If possible I would rate it 6/5. The rooms are very beautiful and have everything you need. The motel is close to the road but there is a row of trees in between so the occasional passing vehicle doesn't disturb too much. It was very nice to be able to listen to the forest from the room at night.”

— Henri T., May 2025
★★★★★

“Highly recommend! Great location, the owners are extremely accommodating, and it was extremely affordable. I also loved that they put together a book for each room that tells you about the rooms, check in/out, places to go, restaurants nearby, history of the area, etc. Great for snowmobiling trips, the trail is right off their parking lot!”

— Kelsey S., February 2025

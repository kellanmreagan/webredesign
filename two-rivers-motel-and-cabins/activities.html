Things to Do in the Western U.P.

Waterfalls, Trails & Local Adventures

We are located in the heart of the Ottawa National Forest, where the Jumbo and the East Branch of the Ontonagon Rivers meet. The Ottawa National Forest offers 953,000 acres with more than 500 lakes, 2,000 miles of streams, and countless waterfalls. Our resort is situated directly on Trail #8 / SB Route, offering easy access to snowmobiling, four-wheeling/side-by-side, and hiking. With an average of 150+ inches of snow annually, and hundreds of miles of groomed snowmobile and cross-country ski trails, there's no reason to hibernate!

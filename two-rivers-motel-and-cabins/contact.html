🚗 Coming by Car?

We're located between Trout Creek, MI & Kenton, MI, just off M-28. You will be turning north at the “Old M-28/Golden Glow Road” road sign.

  • From Bruce Crossing (the West): head east on M-28 about 15 miles, pass through Trout Creek about 2 miles, look for the “Old M-28” road sign and turn left (north).
  • From Marquette (the East): head west on M-28 for ~80 miles, pass through Kenton about 1.5 miles, look for the “Old M-28” road sign and turn right (north).

We are just around the corner after you turn on Old M-28. GPS Coordinates: 46.8193, -88.8722

Tip: look for the Two Rivers Motel & Cabins marquee sign and the old stone chimney across the road—you're in the right place!

🏍 ORV & ❄️ Snowmobile Trail Access

We're located right on Trail #8 (snowmobile) / SB Route (ORV)—a perfect stop for riders! Look for our sign near the trail crossing at Old M-28 between Trout Creek and Kenton.

  • Cell and GPS reception can be spotty in the U.P. — download directions before you drive
  • If you arrive after hours, please check the office (in the front porch of the old farmhouse) for your welcome info

Need help finding us from the trail? Give us a call—we're happy to guide you in.

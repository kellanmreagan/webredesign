Everything you need to know before you book.

💰 Rates & Discounts

Motel Rooms

$95/night (up to 2 guests)

Housekeeping Cabins

$115/night (up to 4 guests)
$20/night for each additional guest (max 8 total)

Optional linens, towels & pillows: $20/night.

Discounts

  • Cash Discount — available for reservations made through Google, our website, and our office
  • Weekly Discount — 10% off stays of 7+ nights
  • Monthly Discount — 20% off stays of 28+ nights
  • Renting the entire property? Call us for a 10% discount.

Payment Methods

We accept cash and all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American Express). We do not accept personal checks.

Additional Fees

  • 6% Michigan sales tax
  • 3% credit card processing fee (if not paying with cash)

For details about how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy and Accessibility Statement.

🛏️ Minimum Stay & Booking Info

  • Cabins require a 2-night stay
  • Motel rooms require a 2-night stay on weekends; a 3-night minimum may apply on holidays
  • For same-day or one-night stays, please call 906-852-3541
  • No deposit is required to make a reservation
  • Credit card info is taken at booking strictly for late cancellations, excessive cleaning due to smoke/damages, and same-day cancellations & no-shows
  • You will receive a confirmation email shortly after booking — didn't get it? Please call us to confirm

❌ Cancellations & Refunds

Cancel 14+ days before arrival

No charge

Cancel within 14 days

50% late cancellation charge applies (minimum one night), unless we can rebook at least 50% of the nights

Cancel day of arrival or no-show

Charged in full

Snowmobile Season Exception: if snowmobile trails are unrideable (at our discretion), cancellation fees are waived.

No refunds for early departure or unused nights. Shortening a reservation within 14 days counts as a cancellation.

🐾 Smoking & Pets

No Smoking Indoors

All units are smoke-free. A $250 cleaning fee will apply if smoking occurs inside.

Pets Welcome — $10/night

Please do not leave pets unattended unless in a cage or portable kennel. Please cover furniture and beds to reduce pet hair. Well-behaved pets can be off leash when outside if attended — please respect other guests who prefer pets on a leash.

🕑 Check-In & Check-Out

Check-In: 3:00 PM

Stop by the office, located in the front porch of the farmhouse.

Check-Out: 10:00 AM

Housekeeping Cabin Checkout

All cabins are house-cleaning cabins. Guests must clean before departure: vacuum, remove trash, wipe counters, tidy kitchen. You do not need to clean the bathroom. Report any issues or damages immediately — guests are responsible for damages beyond normal wear.

📦 Amenities

Included with All Units

  • Free Wi-Fi & DirectTV
  • Microwave, paper towels, and hair dryer
  • Summer air conditioning
  • Access to community fire pit with free firewood
  • Access to indoor and outdoor (seasonal) games

Cabins Also Include

  • Private charcoal grill
  • Fully stocked kitchen — full-sized refrigerator, toaster, coffee maker, crockpot, kitchen towels, pots, pans, plates, cups & silverware
  • Roku TV

Motel Rooms Also Include

  • Shared charcoal grill
  • Single-cup Keurig, K-Cup Pods & apartment-sized refrigerator
  • Linens, towels & pillows provided

Optional linens, towels & pillows for cabins: $20/night.

🚗 Parking for Trucks & Trailers

We have plenty of room for parking your truck and trailer! Whether you are bringing up your ORVs, snowmobiles, or other equipment, there is plenty of room for you to park, unload, and enjoy your time in the U.P.

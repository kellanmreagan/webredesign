Our four cozy motel rooms offer a clean, budget-friendly stay with vintage character and updated essentials. Whether you're stopping through or staying a while, you'll enjoy private baths, Wi-Fi, apartment sized fridges, and easy access to all that Trout Creek and the Western U.P. have to offer.

Meet the Rooms

Motel Room #2 — Snowmobile & ORV Room

  • Sleeps 2 — one queen bed
  • Single cup Keurig with K-Cup Pods, microwave, apartment-sized refrigerator
  • Private shower · Hair dryer
  • Roku TV, Wi-Fi, seasonal A/C
  • Heated by central outdoor wood boiler
  • Shared charcoal grill + fire pit access

Fuel up, gear down, and rest easy. This room is made for those who chase the trail—from sleds in winter to ORVs in summer. With nods to the motorsport culture of the U.P., you'll find snowmobile and ORV decor, and space to stash your gear.

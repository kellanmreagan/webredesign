Welcome to Pleasant Valley Log Cabins!

 

Our cabins are very secluded, with all the comforts of home.

 

Come and hike the hills and valleys of Wisconsin, enjoy a warm crackling fire, a good book and just relax or maybe canoe the Kickapoo, hike Wildcat Mountain State Park, bike the Elroy Sparta Bike trail!  Whatever you decide, you will leave agreeing that ....Pleasant Valley is a wonderful place to be.

 

 

 

Hello!  We are a family business located in the heart of the mid-west that offers log cabin rentals and my wife also works as a teacher in our local school district!

Nestled in Southwestern Wisconsin, near Ontario, our log cabins are very secluded in a woods setting, with hiking, biking, canoeing and horseback riding all within minutes.  Come and canoe the very scenic Kickapoo River, or bike the Elroy Sparta Bike trail.  We are family oriented and would love to have your family visit.  If you have any questions, or want more information please contact us by either phone, mail or e-mail at the following: Phone:  (608) 633-0029    (ask for Joe or LeAnn Fisher)

Pleasant Valley Log Cabins Rental Rates (Policies are at the bottom of page)

  Moose Lodge                  
Our Largest Log Cabin, named Moose Lodge, is fantastic!  It is a 5 bedroom with 2 baths.  There are 2 queen sized beds and 4 full sized beds in the cabin.  It will accommodate up to 12 people.  Located in the woods, it offers a very unique rustic experience.  Privacy, seclusion and the great outdoors, with all the comforts of home including:  a large covered porch, large deck, downstairs bar, DVD TV (no reception), fully furnished kitchen, outside charcoal grill, picnic table and fire ring (linens provided at additional charge). Pets are not permitted in this cabin.  Outside smoking only. (click here for photo gallery of Moose Cabin)

Deer Meadow

The Large Log Cabin, named "Deer Meadow" has a wood burning free standing fireplace, dvd/vcr TV (no reception), fully furnished kitchen, 1 bathroom, 2 bedrooms (queen beds in each), large loft (full bed and 2 twins), large covered porch, outside charcoal grill, picnic table and fire ring (linens provided at additional charge).  It is located in the woods. Pets are not permitted in this cabin. Outside smoking only. (click here for photo gallery of Large Cabin- "Deer Meadow" cabin.)

Bird's Nest 

The Medium Log Cabin, named "The Bird's Nest", has a gas fireplace, fully furnished kitchen, 1 bedroom (1 double bed), 1 bathroom, 2 loft areas accessed by steps(1 double bed and 2 twins), dvd tv (no reception), large covered porch, outside charcoal grill, picnic table and fire ring (linens provided at additional charge).  It is located in the woods. We permit pets in this cabin with prior arrangements.  There is a $15 pet fee (per pet) for a  2 night stay and $5 (per pet) for each additional night.  Outside smoking only. (click here for photo gallery of Medium Cabin- "Bird's Nest" cabin)

Bear's Den

Our Smallest Cabin, named "The Bear's Den" is tri-level, has a fully furnished kitchen, 1 bedroom (1 queen bed), outside charcoal grill and picnic table, fire ring, tv that plays dvd's (no reception) and 1 bathroom.  Perfect for honeymoons and romantic getaways.  Pets are not permitted in this cabin.  Outside smoking only.  (click here for photo gallery of "Bear's Den" cabin)

Policies: 

  •  A security deposit ($150 for log cabins) is required to secure a reservation.  This can be done with a personal check or credit card.

  • In the event of the need to cancel a reservation, the deposit is refundable (less a $25 fee) if the cancellation is done with-in 3 weeks prior to the stay. 

  • The Moose Lodge cabin is a non-refundable reservation and must be paid for in advance.  If multiple cabins are rented, the reservation is non-refundable and must be paid in advance.  Otherwise, payment for the stay is required upon arrival.

  • The security deposit is refunded at check-out (or within 2 weeks of departure if check-out is not performed).

  • Linens are not provided at the above prices.  Linens/Blankets are available at a cost of $10.00/set. 

  • The above prices do not include our local tax. 

  • For the comfort of all, Pleasant Valley does not permit inside smoking. 

  • We do permit pets in our Bird's Nest Cabin Only (with prior arrangement). There is a $15 pet fee (per pet) for a  2 night stay and $5 (per pet) for each additional night.

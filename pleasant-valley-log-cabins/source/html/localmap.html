Directions to Pleasant Valley Log Cabins

 

 

Directions to Pleasant Valley Log Cabins from Ontario, WI 

(These directions are best used when driving from Iowa or Minnesota)

 

-Go East on State Hwy. 33 for ½ mile and turn left on Cty. ZZ.  Go 3.1 miles and turn left onto Cty. Z. Go 2 miles and turn right onto 24th Ct/Oregano Road.  Go 1.1 miles and your destination is on your right.  The Address of the cabin entrance is S453 24th Ct. 

 

Directions to Pleasant Valley Log Cabins from Hillsboro, WI

(These directions are best used when driving from Madison/Chicago/Green Bay direction)

-Go West on State Hwy. 33 for approximately 6 miles.  Turn right onto Cty. Highway V.  Go 2.3 miles and turn left onto County Hwy. F.  Go 0.3 miles and turn left onto Cty. Hwy F.  Go 1.9 miles and turn right onto 24th Ct. (also labeled Oregano Road on other end).  Go 0.8 miles and your destination is on your left.  The address of the cabin entrance is S453 24th Ct.

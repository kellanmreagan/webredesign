Fishing at Our Outposts

Rawhide Lake

Rawhide Lake is a fly-in or portage-in lake. A crystal clear lake offering excellent lake trout fishing combined with smallmouth bass fishing.

Rawhide Lake is a fly-in or portage-in lake. Rawhide is a crystal clear lake offering excellent lake trout fishing combined with smallmouth bass fishing. This camp is designed like a Motel with 5 housekeeping units of different sizes to sleep 2 - 6 persons as well as the original old log norway house that sleeps 6. These units are equipped with bunks, propane stoves, fridge and lights, wood stoves & wood for heath. We also provide cooking utensils, blankets, linens and pillows. There are barbecues centrally located and a screened fish cleaning station.

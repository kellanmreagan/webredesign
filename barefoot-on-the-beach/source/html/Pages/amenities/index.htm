Amenities

Beach
Just steps west of the resort is Bonita Beach, a beautiful sandy white beach on the Gulf of Mexico. Charcoal grills are available for your use.
Tennis The Club's har-tru clay tennis courts offer a complete program with organized play, tournaments, and private lessons. Tennis is free if staying at our condo. Please call 239-992-2345 for more tennis information.  
Swimming
Two heated swimming pools that are always inviting, refreshing, and relaxing are available.
  
Bocci Ball
Join in the Bocci Ball leagues and make new friends.
  
Basketball
A basketball hoop is available east of the tennis courts. A basketball can be checked out from the manager's office.
  
Golf
Bring your own clubs and balls to enjoy the chipping green which is located just west of the tennis courts.
 
Barbecue Grills
Two gas barbecue grills are located near the tennis court entrance.
  
Schuffleboard
Schuffleboard is on site and equipment is available in the lobby.
  
Horseshoes
Horseshoes are on site and equipment is available in the lobby

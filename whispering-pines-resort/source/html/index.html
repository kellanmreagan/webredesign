positive feedback. We want everyone to enjoy themselves and pass the word.

      W.P.R. is our little corner of northern Michigan's great outdoors. We have four log cabin style units located right on the upper Manistee river. We also have a small campground with water and electric hookups for RVs. Though the cabins were originally built in the late 1940's their recent renovation has created a cozy if not romantic indoor retreat.

All the cabins have cast iron gas log fireplaces, fully equipped kitchens, cable TV and Wi-Fi.

Early spring is the time for flyfishing on the Manistee. Thats the best time to hit this section of the river.

      As time moves into the summer most people come up to canoe or kayak. This is a beginner level paddling river though the upper reaches can require some tight manuevering you can walk across on your knees in most places. It maintains a cool temperature throughout the summer

Late fall brings hunters and get-away weekenders.

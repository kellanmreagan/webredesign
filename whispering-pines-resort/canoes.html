On the river

Canoe Liveries

The river is crystal clear and quite cool most of the time. We have several canoes and kayaks for free use by our guests. We do not rent our boats to the public.

Spotting service

We charge $5/kayak & $10/canoe to spot anyone's boat at any of our usual put-ins. Ask the manager to make arrangements for long distance spotting.

The scenery on the upper Manistee River is remote, pristine and relaxing.

Since the 1930s

It's been a beautiful transformation

Whispering Pines has been around in one form or another since the 1930's and has had many owners. In its present state it is the third and fourth of four parcels divided from the original resort acreage. We acquired parcel #4 in 2001 from the legendary fishing guide Frank Love, following his death in 2000. His memory lives on by the river. The four older cabins on the adjacent parcel #2 were the first cabins built and are now privately owned, as is parcel #1.

We became the new owners in June of 1997 and we have been very busy ever since. Tearing out everything and exposing the rough-sawn wood of the ceilings was the first big step. We then brought in cedar and pine logs to reinforce the roofs and build the lofts. We've refinished all four units and they look great inside and out.

We are located on the Manistee River

5 miles west of the small town of Frederic in the northern lower peninsula of Michigan off County Road 612, approximately 3 hours north of Detroit by I-75.

We are right on the river

Map & Directions

We are right on the Upper Manistee river — you can't get closer! If you get lost, call us at (989) 348-2044.

  • From northbound I-75, exit #264 (2 exits north of Grayling #254) and turn left (this is Co. Rd. 612), drive 2mi. to the stop sign at Old 27, turn left, go 200', then right on Co. Rd. 612 again and drive 5mi. where you will cross the Manistee river bridge and see our sign on the left. Turn left and follow the drive until you see our office sign.
  • From US27 northbound, stay on US27 until it joins northbound I-75 just north of Higgins Lake, then follow northbound I-75 to exit #264 (2 exits north of the Grayling exit #254) and turn left (this is Co. Rd. 612), drive 2mi. to the stop sign at Old 27, turn left, go 200', then right on Co. Rd. 612 again and drive 5mi. where you will cross the Manistee river bridge and see our sign on the left. Turn left and follow the drive until you see our office sign.
  • From US131 northbound, follow US131 north to 72 east, just south of Kalkaska, turn right (east) on 72 and continue east until Manistee River Road, which is about 7mi west of Grayling, turn left (north) on Manistee River Road, continue on to the stop sign at 612. Turn left (west) for approx. 500ft. where you will cross the Manistee river bridge and see our sign on the left. Turn left and follow the drive until you see our office sign.
  • From M72, take Manistee River road (it goes north only) to the stop sign at Co. Rd. 612 (6mi.) and turn left, cross the bridge and see our sign on the left. Turn left and follow the drive until you see our office sign.

If you get lost… call us at (989) 348-2044
